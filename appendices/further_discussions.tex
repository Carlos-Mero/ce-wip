\section{Further Discussions}
\label{app:further-discussions}

Our analysis of variance accumulation concerns entropy evolution during
prolonged language-model training. More broadly, it raises the possibility
that stochastic fluctuations can induce a deterministic drift toward lower
diversity in other iterative systems, including biological evolution and
technological development. For example, even when two technological
solutions are equally effective, random fluctuations in their early
exploration and adoption could become self-reinforcing, leaving one
insufficiently explored and producing a less diverse outcome. Such a
mechanism could contribute to path dependence without requiring an
intrinsic difference in the quality of the alternatives.

These connections remain speculative. Establishing whether variance
accumulation operates in such systems, and distinguishing its effects
from other mechanisms, would require domain-specific model and empirical
evidence that are difficult to obtain and beyond the scope of this work.
We mention these possibilities only to suggest broader directions for
investigating the phenomenon.
