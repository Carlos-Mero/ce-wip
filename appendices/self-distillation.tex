\section{Supplementary Experiments on Proximal and Distal Distillation}
\label{app:distillation-experiments}

We report supplementary results for distillation from identical, nearby,
and more distant teachers.

\subsection{Experimental settings}

The proximal-limit experiment initializes both student and frozen teacher
from Qwen3-1.7B-Base~\citep{yang_qwen3_2025}. Nearby teachers are obtained by training the same
model with GRPO for 50, 100, or 200 steps. The distal experiments use a
JustRL teacher with R1-Distill-1.5B~\citep{deepseek-ai_deepseek-r1_2025} and Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B~\citep{qwen_qwen25_2025} students;
both use the same cached teacher responses for SFT. Training uses slime
with Megatron and SGLang at revision \texttt{a067ce6f}, with:
\begin{itemize}
  \item seed: 1;
  \item Qwen3 experiments: DAPO17k~\citep{yu_dapo_2025}, maximum response length 2,048;
  \item JustRL experiments: JustRL training data~\citep{he_justrl_2025}, maximum response length 8,192;
  \item learning rate: $10^{-5}$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Distillation from nearby teachers.} With each of the three GRPO-trained teachers, OPD achieves a higher mean
evaluation score than SFT (Table~\ref{tab:distillation-performance}),
consistent with its proximal advantage in these single-seed experiments.

\paragraph{Distillation with a larger initial performance gap.} With the JustRL teacher, both methods bring R1-Distill-1.5B close to
teacher performance, with SFT slightly ahead. For Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B,
OPD rollout correctness declines and remains low
(Figure~\ref{fig:distillation-distal}), while SFT improves the mean
evaluation score from 15.27 to 47.46 (Table~\ref{tab:distillation-performance}).

\subsection{Comparision with Iterative Methods}

Figure~\ref{fig:distillation-proximal-limit} shows zero recorded gradient
norm for both OPD runs and nonzero sampled gradients for SFT, consistent
with Lemma~\ref{lem:proximal-gradient-noise}. Neither iterative self-OPD
nor proximal SFT exhibits entropy collapse.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{media/distillation_comparison.pdf}
  \caption{ The training dynamics of different self-distillation variants. We can see that neither iterative self-OPD nor proximal SFT experienced entropy collapse. The nonzero proximal variance and iteration are two necessasy conditions for variance accumulation to occur. RL is where both conditions are satisfied. These experiments were conducted on Qwen3-1.7B-Base with a learning rate of $10^{-5}$ with AdamW optimizer.}
  \label{fig:distillation-proximal-limit}
\end{figure}
